# Two-speaker separation on the decoded receive audio

Try the public 16 kHz WHAMR SepFormer trained for reverberant two-speaker mixtures. Use the published two-output ONNX conversion on the RTX 4090. Use all 15 recorded early-marker weak-room sends, eight previously recorded quiet two-sentence Nyx/Willow sends and the known unaddressed 072 send. Evaluate both service readiness settings. Decode the archived P4 Opus packets and compare every PCM hash to the original receiver result. The model used English speech and reverberation in training. The model card does not establish conversion parity with the original PyTorch checkpoint. [Upstream model](https://huggingface.co/speechbrain/sepformer-whamr16k) · [ONNX conversion](https://huggingface.co/tonythethompson/SepFormer-WhamR16k-ONNX/tree/3f43d502c36e3c26747350d44aeb487615ed01a5)

Read both complete source tracks without an ASR prompt. Select one before reading its words. Use the greater ratio of mean squared energy from 0.4 to 2.4 seconds after the measured first open to the energy in the one second immediately before it. Use an energy floor of 1e−10 in normalized float PCM and break ties by the first output index. The delayed stream's inserted silence counts toward its receive clock. Quantize each output to PCM16 at the model output level, applying one common downward gain to both tracks only if needed to prevent clipping. Compare selected, either-track (oracle upper bound) and original complete-command suffixes for each reader, including losses on quiet controls. An unaddressed command transcribing successfully is tracked separately. This is an offline received-audio experiment.
