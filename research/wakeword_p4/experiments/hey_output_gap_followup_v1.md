counterparts. Keep held IDs, development/acceptance assignments and original
audio boundaries. Do not infer physical-speaker or pretraining independence
from different model names or hashes. Any new recipe must state which factors
change; broad-source versus two-voice comparisons alone do not isolate diversity.
