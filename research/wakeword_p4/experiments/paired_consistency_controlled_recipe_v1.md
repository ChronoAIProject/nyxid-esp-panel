# Proposed controlled intervention: same-source rendering consistency

Status: concrete proposal and preflight specification, not permission to skip preflight or a running training experiment. V46/v47 remain rejected. Mac original selected-v46 full-grid measurements (0015593) and GPU frozen diagnostics support testing rendering sensitivity; neither proves this intervention effective.

## Hypothesis and single difference

Adding same-source quiet-to-rendered representation consistency to supervised training may reduce noise-induced classifier-input changes while retaining near-word discrimination. Use a consistency term rather than target-class contrastive attraction for this first intervention: it does not require a single-keyword multi-class denominator, additional margins/temperature, or forcing different target conditioning IDs into one cluster. The mathematical counterexample in 4bfb3fe rules out treating nonzero toy gradients as an anti-collapse proof.

Run one matched control and one treatment, each exactly 120000 updates, once, without a sweep. Both start from the original reconstructed teacher60 initialization and seed726 classifier reset, context150, frozen BN, original P4 inference graph, batch128 and inherited v47 main sampling/weights/Adam schedule/checkpoint/export protocol. Neither starts from selected v46/v47 weights. Both add the exact same 40 supervised paired windows each update. The only arm difference is consistency coefficient zero versus the one coefficient fixed by the initialization-only rule below. Consequently this estimates the added consistency effect under the paired-BCE recipe; it does not isolate the effect of adding paired BCE relative to historical v47.

## Pairing, roles and loss

Use the authenticated40320 training-only raw pack. An independent seed227726 chooses one speed uniformly, two distinct allowed numeric conditioning IDs uniformly without replacement, all four phrases and five arms. Use one endpoint uniformly from the shared legal phase1 set for all40 views, exactly as e3e701f. This is two synthetic conditioning IDs, not a claim of two physical speakers. Preserve all sources and conditions, no score mining/filtering. Existing main128 draws and RNG streams remain unchanged. Both arms draw identical ordered tuples and endpoints on every update.

Main loss stays mean over128 of original weighted binary BCE (target1, negatives20). Pair loss is mean over40 of BCE with those same label weights and coefficient1, identical in both arms. This adds positive10 and negative30 windows to each arm (two IDs times five arms of Nyx, and two IDs times three near words times five arms); it is an explicit supervised mass change shared by the control. Total supervised loss is main mean plus pair mean, not a silently reweighted168-example mean. The initial proposal's positive8/negative32 count was a bookkeeping error corrected before any training; 32 is the number of rendering consistency pairs, not negative windows.

For each of the8 ID/phrase sources, quiet and its four alternate renderings are positive view pairs. Let e be the full original300-dim Flatten input to the unchanged head, u=e/max(norm(e),1e-8). D=mean over32 pairs of (1-dot(u_quiet,u_rendered)). Gradients flow to both views. No stop-gradient teacher, new projection head, temporal pooling or classifier normalization. Unit normalization applies only inside D; raw e and the original affine sigmoid head feed BCE and deployment. Norm/zero counts and raw classifier logits remain observable.

Nyx, Nick, Next and Mix each have their own same-source rendering pairs. Distinct conditioning IDs and distinct phrases are never consistency positives. There are no explicit contrastive negatives or Unknown anchor groups in D. Near words receive label0/weight20 through both main and pair BCE; broad Unknown/background stays in the inherited main BCE and is never collapsed into a positive contrastive cluster. No cross-ID same-target attraction in this minimal first intervention. Two IDs provide sampling diversity, not another loss term.

Treatment loss=L_main_BCE+L_pair_BCE+lambda*D. Control computes the same forwards and D for observation but uses lambda0; one shared optimizer update per arm per batch. BN remains frozen in both passes. Extra forward/backward budget and ordering must match; record both gradient norms and effective optimizer updates. No deployment graph or state-buffer change.

## Fixed coefficient rule and mandatory preflight

Before training or reading any new data, replay the first50 main+pair batches at the shared original initialization without optimizer updates. Compute encoder-only trainable-variable gradients for supervised L_main+L_pair and D, their norms and cosine, per-layer and aggregate; record head gradients separately (D must not connect to the head). Define R=median(||g_supervised_encoder||)/median(||g_D_encoder||). Require both finite and positive. Choose lambda=min(1, 10**floor(log10(0.1*R))). This power-of-ten rule targets at most one tenth of the median supervised encoder norm before optimizer clipping; it is a scale heuristic, not a claim of optimality. Record resulting per-batch ratios, gradient conflict, and main/pair BCE contributions. Freeze one resulting lambda in a subsequent execution receipt before either run. Do not adjust it from loss curves, old/new development results, collapse observations or failed acceptance. If gradients are disconnected/nonfinite/zero, stop the proposal and report rather than rescuing it with a sweep.

Before that real-initialization probe: independently check analytic/finite-difference gradients for the normalized consistency loss; exact pairing and 50-block ordered replay; no cross-phrase pairs; head disconnection; full BCE/regularizer gradient connectivity; no optimizer or BN/weight mutation; stable behavior for equal views and tiny/zero vectors. Explicitly demonstrate collapsed representations can be stationary for D. Retaining BCE does not prove global anti-collapse: zero classifier weights and bias at weighted class prior can make a completely constant representation stationary too. Preserve that counterexample rather than advertising a guarantee. A finite noncollapsed example must show binary labels give different representation gradients under a nonzero head. Actual gradients/scale on the frozen initialization are required; a2D example is insufficient.

Only after this code/gradient/initialization/sampler preflight and the concrete execution pins are delivered through Issue13 may the two predeclared runs begin. This is an engineering sequencing condition, not a user approval request. Mac can independently reproduce formula and scale before expensive runs; no unbounded wait for optional feedback.
