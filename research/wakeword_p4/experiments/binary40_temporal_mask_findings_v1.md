# Binary40 temporal supervision audit

The original sampler already uses aligned command-prefix references through `causal_aligned_prefix_batch_v1.py`; proposing prefix alignment again would repeat prior work. Earlier temporal-tail-risk, bare-support and latent-command experiments also exist. No new training is selected by this audit.

The immutable first128 training-fit archive was authenticated and inspected without inference or optimization. All32 positive rows have one contiguous positive region of8 or9 calls; all32 have no negative-weight supervision after that region. In the shared mask implementation, positive support is container-end+480 through container-end+4320samples inclusive; negative support for positive examples stops at the lead boundary. Positive and negative regions are independently normalized, and presentation BCE weights the latter20. This is an implementation description, not evidence that a particular coefficient causes misses.

The post-positive tensor includes padding, so its score counts cannot be treated as acoustic-tail duration or physical decoder rearm. This small batch overlaps calibration in its first16 rows and does not establish population coverage. Full isolated broad-fit positives also need not predict repeated wake performance. Existing cold-cache isolation does not test carry from a preceding utterance.

Next bounded evidence: rescore the already saved continuous raw codes with the fixed five-score event state machine, accounting for every wake container as (a) no threshold crossing, (b) crossing blocked by refractory, (c) crossing while not rearmed, or (d) emitted event, including carries across boundaries. Authenticate the original traces and clocks. This is descriptive consumed diagnostic evidence only: do not tune threshold/rearm/refractory, add failed clips to training or infer phonetic/first-command timing. If loss is predominantly absent crossings rather than decoder blocking, a repeated-wake/rearm training proposal is not supported by these traces. A distinct training proposal must first pass this mechanism check and the historical-recipe review.
