# Prospective source-role reservation contract

Before any new synthesis, authenticate a historical consumed-identity ledger with voice IDs, model SHA256 and known physical-speaker IDs. Explicitly document ledger coverage limits; a matching hash proves integrity, not completeness. Reconcile the ledger against earlier experiments/source reviews before calling a holdout independent. Unknown physical speaker and pretraining overlap remain unknown.

Plan schema `synthetic-role-reservation-v1` supplies `consumed_ledger` path/SHA256/coverage_limits, all three roles in `requests`, and failure_policy `retain-all-stop-pack-no-substitution`. Each request has unique request_id, role, voice_id, physical_speaker_id (nullable), model/config path/SHA256, upstream URL/revision, runtime_versions, text, pronunciation_input, native_rate, positive finite scales, native_return_saved_before_transform=true, trimmed=false, duration_metadata_interface, phoneme_metadata_interface and pretraining_overlap_status. Do not invent exposed timing/seed information. Reusing a voice/model/known speaker across roles fails; known consumed identities cannot become development/acceptance.

`audit_synthetic_role_reservation_v1.py` performs file/identity checks before generation, without invoking TTS or detectors. A pass alone does not establish provenance or independence. Native output must then pass existing synthetic_output_quality_v1, and augmented output existing complete-source/RIR/noise/gain audits. No new source is reserved or generated by this contract itself. Fixed v30/candidate/official reference comparison, unconsumed threshold development and independent acceptance remain required. Historical consumed finals stay closed. No human listening prerequisite.
