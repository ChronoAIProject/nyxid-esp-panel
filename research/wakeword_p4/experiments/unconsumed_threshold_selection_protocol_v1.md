# Prospective threshold selection rule

This component freezes the selection algorithm before any new development scores. It does not reserve sources, authorize new generation by itself, or admit consumed Jenny/Joe/legacy data. No threshold was selected while writing it. A future execution plan must bind the actual untouched development manifest, its recorded historical use, quality reports, one candidate SHA256, selector source hash, decoder and continuous-background manifest before scoring. Unknown upstream pretraining/speaker overlap must be reported; it is not automatically proof of leakage or a requirement to prove universally absent overlap.

Score one frozen candidate on the complete development input population, preserving all original UINT8 steps and clocks. Use existing five-step arithmetic smoothing,67-step refractory,half-threshold rearm and81600zeroPCM continuous prime through frontend/model/decoder; preserve state and priming events, suppress notifications only. No crop/readiness censoring or per-threshold input differences. Replay the event policy over rawcodes at each exact binary threshold k/1280,k=1..1280, covering all attainable five-UINT8-mean score boundaries. This is one prospective development grid, not independent evidence at1280points. Do not assume event counts monotone because rearm/cooldown may change event identities.

Declare positive strata before scores as voice×bare/connected-command. Every expected stratum must have positive exposure; no dropping difficult voices or utterances after inspection. Count any post-source app wake as a hit, report generated-prefix/whole-container timing separately as descriptive process references, never automatic human phonetic truth. Confusion metric is activated clips over the complete prospectively specified nearword/common-negative population. Background metric is all actual notifications over full continuous real exposure; priming notifications are separately retained.

A threshold is feasible for selection only if exact one-sided95% Poisson FAR upper bound chi2.ppf(.95,2*(events+1))/(2*hours) <=0.5/h. If none passes, report no feasible point; never borrow repeated background exposure, silently append hours or relax the bound. Among feasible points maximize minimum positive-stratum hit fraction, then overall positive hit fraction, then minimize confusion activated clips, then prefer the higher threshold. This explicitly prioritizes worst voice/carrier sensitivity subject to the stated background bound; it does not guarantee acceptable confusion or sensitivity. Preserve the entire table, tie outcomes and zero-hit strata.

`select_unconsumed_threshold_v1.py` validates the complete ordered grid and fixed denominators, consumes a metric table, and records its hash and selector hash. It does not load models/audio or establish freshness from a role string alone. An execution plan and actual source-use evidence must establish freshness externally. Development FAR feasibility is not independent final qualification, and the selector always emits promotion_qualified:false. Freeze the chosen threshold before untouched final scoring; final acceptance protocol/officialHiESP and v30 matched inputs remain required. If selected sensitivity/confusion fails final gates, keep v30 and all failures. No new perfect-zero-confusion gate is introduced by this rule.
