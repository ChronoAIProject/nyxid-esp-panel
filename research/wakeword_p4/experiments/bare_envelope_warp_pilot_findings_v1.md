Ratios reach the fixed cap in both directions; these are not imperceptibility or pronunciation-preservation measures.

This is an explicitly local spectral-envelope perturbation, not an implementation claimed equivalent to canonical VTLP, pure formant shifting, or the historical waveform pitch experiment. Multiplying a smoothed envelope differs mechanically from moving the entire spectrum, but it does not prove measured F0 is unchanged or that the distinguishing vowel/consonants survive. RMS matching also changes gain. Fullwave transformation affects background and watermark as well as speech. Do not call derivatives new independent voices.

Search of tracked research found historical v10 SpecAugment, scalar affine MFCC style augmentation, per-draw noise and C161 pitch experiments; their failures remain. The author homepage https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~ndjaitly/ links jaitly-icml13.pdf as an audio-data augmentation paper. The present pilot tests its documented local algorithm only.

Next decision is conditional, not a preauthorized fit: quantify whether the envelope manipulation preserves harmonic structure and short-word information on training-only positives AND matched lexical negatives, preserving original labels and reporting ambiguity. A training intervention would require exact same-source positive/negative transformation support, complete waveform/frontend provenance, independent sampler RNG, fixed source budgets and comparison against saved C161. Do not admit quarantined clips, select transform parameters on Joe/Mac/British development failures, or repeat augmentation strengths until consumed metrics improve. No long training is justified solely by this successful mechanical pilot.
